Tourist Places in karnataka

Immerse yourself in the serene landscapes, vibrant culture, and rich traditions.

Bangalore

Bangalore, also known as the Garden City, is known for many special things,
including its food, parks, malls, and shopping

Lal Bagh* - A sprawling botanical garden known for its glass house and flower shows. *Bangalore Palace* - A Tudor-style architectural marvel resembling Windsor Castle. *Bannerghatta National Park* - A wildlife sanctuary home to lions, tigers, and a butterfly park.

mysore

Mysore is a city in Karnataka, India that is known for its palaces, gardens, and rich heritage.

. *Mysore Palace* - A stunning royal palace famous for its grandeur and illumination during Dussehra. *Chamundi Hill* - A hilltop temple dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari, offering panoramic views of Mysore. *Brindavan Gardens* - Famous for its symmetric design and musical fountain shows near the Krishnarajasagar Dam.

coorg

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is known for its natural beauty, eco-friendly lifestyle,
and many attractions.

*Abbey Falls* - A scenic waterfall surrounded by lush greenery. *Dubare Elephant Camp* - A place to interact with and learn about elephants. *Raja’s Seat* - A viewpoint offering stunning views of misty valleys and mountains.

hampi

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is known for its natural beauty, eco-friendly lifestyle, and
many attractions .

*Virupaksha Temple* - An ancient Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, dating back to the 7th century.
*Vittala Temple* - Renowned for its stone chariot and musical pillars.
*Stone Chariot* - A symbolic monument, one of India's most iconic structures.

chikmagalur

Chikmagalur, a destination in Karnataka, is known for its beautiful landscapes,
cultural heritage, and a variety of attractions.

*Mullayanagiri* - The highest peak in Karnataka, ideal for trekking and nature views.
*Baba Budangiri* - Known for its caves and scenic mountain views, with religious significance for both Hindus and Muslims.
*Coffee Plantations* - Famous for its coffee estates, offering tours and tastings.

gokarna

it is Known for its idyllic beaches and pristine waters, Gokarna is a
favorite among travelers who seek a peaceful vacation.

*Om Beach* - A uniquely shaped beach resembling the symbol "Om".
*Kudle Beach* - A peaceful, less crowded beach ideal for relaxation.
*Mahabaleshwar Temple* - A revered Shiva temple known for its Atmalinga.

udupi

Udupi, a coastal town in Karnataka, India, is known for its temples, beaches,
cuisine, and cultural heritage.

*Sri Krishna Temple* - A famous Hindu temple known for its Krishna idol and unique worship through a window.
*Malpe Beach* - A popular beach for water sports, known for its serene beauty.

jog falls

bijapur

Bijapur, Karnataka is known for many famous places.

*Gol Gumbaz* - Known for its massive dome, it's the mausoleum of Mohammed Adil Shah.
*Ibrahim Rauza* - A beautifully designed tomb and mosque complex, often referred to as the "Taj Mahal of the Deccan."

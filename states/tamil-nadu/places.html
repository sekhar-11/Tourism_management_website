Tourist Places in tamil nadu

Immerse yourself in the serene landscapes, vibrant culture, and rich traditions.

chennai

The capital city with beaches like Marina Beach, and historical sites like
Fort St. George, Kapaleeshwarar Temple, and San Thome Basilica.

Chennai is a culture-rich city with carnatic music, delicious food, serene beaches and popular temples. It is one of the most preferred tourist destinations in India. The city never fails to mesmerise its visitors as it offers a unique experience.

Mahabalipuram

-Famous for its UNESCO World Heritage-listed rock-cut temples,
especially the Shore Temple, Five Rathas, and Arjuna’s Penance..

It is known especially for its rathas (temples in the form of chariots), mandapas (cave sanctuaries), giant open-air reliefs such as the famous 'Descent of the Ganges', and the temple of Rivage, with thousands of sculptures to the glory of Shiva.

Kanchipuram

Known as the "City of a Thousand Temples," it's famous for its stunning temples like
Kamakshi Amman Temple and Kanchi Kailasanathar Temple, and Kanchipuram silk sarees.

Known as the City of Thousand Temples, Kanchipuram is known for its temple architectures, 1000-pillared halls, huge temple towers and silk saris. Kanchipuram serves as one of the most important inland tourist destinations in India. Kanchipuram has become a centre of attraction for foreign tourists as well.

Pondicherry

Though a Union Territory, it's surrounded by Tamil Nadu. Known for its
French colonial architecture, Aurobindo Ashram, and serene beaches.

Fondly called the French Riviera of the East, Pondicherry is a French-inspired Union Territory tucked away in Southern India, yet has a unique charm of its own. The city provides its visitors with a calm, quiet, peaceful atmosphere and some of the best places to stay in Pondicherry all year round.

Madura

Home to the magnificent Meenakshi Amman Temple, and a
city rich in Tamil culture, history, and heritage.

Madurai is famous for its temples built by Pandyan and Madurai Nayak kings in the ancient traditional Tamilian style of architecture. It is also one of India's most outstanding Hindu pilgrimage centres.

Rameswaram

A sacred pilgrimage site, part of the Char Dham, with Ramanathaswamy
Temple and Pamban Bridge.

Rameswaram is significant for many Hindus as a pilgrimage to Varanasi is considered to be incomplete without a pilgrimage to Rameswaram. The town along with the Ramanathaswamy temple is one of the holiest Hindu Char Dham (four divine sites) sites comprising Badrinath, Puri, and Dwarka.

Kanyakumari

The southernmost tip of India, known for its unique sunset and sunrise views,
Vivekananda Rock Memorial, and Thiruvalluvar Statue.

Kanyakumari, formerly known as Cape Comorin, is a coastal town in the Indian state of Tamil Nadu. It is the southernmost tip of mainland India. Kanyakumari is popular for its beautiful beaches, unique Vivekananda Rock Memorial, and stunning sunrise and sunset views. It is also a major pilgrimage center for Hindus.

Thanjavur

Famous for the Brihadeeswarar Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage
site, and Tanjore art and painting.

Thanjavur district is called 'The Rice Bowl of Tamil Nadu' because of its agricultural activities in the delta region of river Cauvey. The temple, culture and architecture of Thanjavur are famous throughout world.

Ooty

A popular hill station in the Nilgiris, known for its tea, gardens, botanical
gardens, and the Nilgiri Mountain Railway (toy train)..

Beat the heat of summer with the pleasant weather of Ooty from March to June; this is the best time to visit Ooty. Go sightseeing at Avalanche Lake, Botanical Garden, and Ooty Lake. The temperature of this place ranges between 20°C-25°C throughout the summer season

Kodaikanal

Another beautiful hill station, known for its misty lakes, waterfalls,
and pine forests. The star-shaped Kodai Lake is a major attraction.

Kodaikanal is also popularly known as the princes of Hill Stations. The cool and misty weather, the scenic beauty of the rolling hills and the wooded forest of Kodaikanal and its surroundings will mesmerize any visitor throughout the year.
